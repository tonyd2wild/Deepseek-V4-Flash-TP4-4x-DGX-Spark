Unofficial community recipe to serve DeepSeek-V4-Flash across FOUR NVIDIA DGX Spark (GB10) nodes as one tensor-parallel group (TP=4) with vLLM over a switched 200GbE RoCE/RDMA fabric. On the Spark's fast fabric, TP=4 is FASTER on single-stream decode than TP=2 (memory-bandwidth-bound decode + fast RoCE all-reduce), with a ~6M-token KV pool and enough concurrency to serve several agent sessions from one endpoint. Includes the launcher, RDMA/NCCL setup, C1-C6 speed benchmarks, and the gotchas. Sibling of the TP=2 recipe: github.com/tonyd2wild/Deepseek-v4-Flash-TP2-DGX-Spark-500k-CTX
